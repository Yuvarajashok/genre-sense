Frequently asked questions

Answers from a user point of view, focused on how Genre Sense behaves while you upload, review, save, and manage your analyses.

What can I upload?

You can upload common image formats such as JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP, and AVIF.

What does Genre Sense actually predict?

It predicts the most likely content genre of your image, such as educational, informational, entertainment, emotional, or promotional.

Why do I see multiple likely genres?

Some images naturally fit more than one category. The app shows ranked probabilities so you can see close alternatives instead of only one hard label.

Does the app read text from my image?

Yes. If your image contains readable text, OCR is used alongside visual features to improve the prediction.

Why is the confidence not 100%?

Confidence reflects how strongly the model believes in a result. Similar genres or unclear visuals usually lower the score.

Can I save my previous analyses?

Yes. Every prediction is stored in your history so you can review it later, filter it, or remove it.

Can I delete old history entries?

Yes. You can delete individual records or select multiple records and remove them in one action from the History page.

Can I set a preferred genre?

Yes. In Profile, you can choose a default genre preference that is highlighted across your prediction workflow.

Can I change my username or email?

Yes, from the Profile page. Those changes update the current device session and are reflected immediately in the UI.

Can I upload a profile picture?

Yes. Your profile picture is stored through the existing upload endpoint and shown in the shared app header.

Why does a poster sometimes classify as promotional instead of informational?

The model weighs both design cues and text intent. If the image looks sales-driven or campaign-style, promotional can outrank informational.

Is dark mode available?

Yes. Use the theme toggle on the login page or inside the app sidebar to switch between light and dark mode.
